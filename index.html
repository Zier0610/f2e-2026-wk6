Vegetables

Crisp, sweet, and naturally vibrant, carrots are a versatile root vegetable rich in beta-carotene and essential vitamins. Perfect for snacking raw, roasting, or adding to soups and salads for a natural crunch.

Plump and full of natural sweetness, sweet corn is a wholesome favorite packed with dietary fiber and essential nutrients. Enjoy it freshly boiled, grilled on the cob, or mixed into delicious side dishes.

Packed with vitamin C and antioxidants, green bell peppers offer a refreshingly mild, slightly tangy flavor and a satisfying crunch. An ideal ingredient for stir-fries, fajitas, or fresh garden salads.
